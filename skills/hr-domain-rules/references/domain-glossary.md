# Domain Glossary

## Employee

The primary person record in the system. Holds identity, contact, employment, and relationship information used by the rest of the platform.

## User

An application login account. A user may be linked to an employee record.

## Manager

The direct reporting owner for an employee in v1. This relationship is used for leave approvals.

## Asset

A company-owned item such as a laptop or phone.

## Asset Assignment

The relationship between an asset and an employee, including assignment and return state.

## Leave Type

A category of leave, such as annual leave or sick leave.

## Leave Balance

The tracked amount of leave available, used, and remaining for an employee.

## Leave Request

A request submitted by an employee for time off during a specific date range.

## Approval

The manager action that changes a leave request from pending to approved or rejected.
